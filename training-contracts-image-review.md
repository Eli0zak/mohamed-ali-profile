## Initial curation direction

The strongest primary images so far are the lobby portrait beside the flags and the MEC Academy speaking-stage image. The classroom training image is also useful, but it should be framed as documentary proof rather than a headline visual. Group photos are better suited to a secondary gallery or a partnerships strip because they communicate scale and collaboration but are visually busier and include more third-party faces.
